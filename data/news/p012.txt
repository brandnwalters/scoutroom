[Kalorska Sport — 3 April 2026]
Hofmann plays every minute again
Tomas Hofmann has completed all 3,060 available league minutes for FK Zeljava, his second consecutive ever-present season. The 22-year-old goalkeeper is contracted until 2027 and has attracted interest from clubs in wealthier divisions.

[Transfer Signal — 20 June 2026]
Goalkeeper valuations are a guessing game, and Hofmann proves it
At €19.5m, Tomas Hofmann is priced as a coming force. Whether that is right is genuinely difficult to say — the statistics that travel well for outfield players travel badly for keepers, and the shot-stopping metrics that matter most are noisy over a single season. Scouts who have watched him in person rate him highly. Scouts who have only seen the data have no strong opinion, which is itself informative about the limits of the data.
