Almeida strikes again as Ashcombe edge Kestrel Park
Rafa Almeida scored his fifteenth of the season, a scuffed six-yard finish that squirmed under the goalkeeper, to give Ashcombe United a 1-0 win. It was not a goal that will feature in any montage, but Almeida's supporters will argue that is rather the point.

[The Terrace Weekly — 3 April 2026]
Almeida's agent: "He is a top-four striker and should be paid like one"
Contract talks between Ashcombe United and Rafa Almeida have stalled, with the 27-year-old's representative Vittorio Lanza telling reporters his client "has outscored every striker in this league not named Okafor and is being offered terms befitting a squad player." Ashcombe are understood to consider the current valuation of €52m to be a floor rather than a ceiling in any negotiation.

[Analytics Corner — 19 April 2026]
The finishing-luck watchlist: five players due a correction
Top of our list, by some distance, is Ashcombe's Rafa Almeida. Eighteen goals from 9.4 expected is the largest overperformance in the division and the third largest we have on record in five seasons of tracking this league. Overperformance of this scale is not repeatable — historically, players in this bracket regress by roughly 60% of the gap the following season. Almeida is a good striker having an exceptional year. Buyers should be pricing the former.
