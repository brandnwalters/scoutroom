[Costeira Diário — 11 February 2026]
Bakayoko's drought reaches nine matches
Idris Bakayoko has now gone nine league matches without a goal for CF Estorilha, a run that has begun to draw impatience from sections of the support. The 23-year-old winger has had 41 shots in that period.

[Analytics Corner — 19 April 2026]
The inverse watchlist: five players being punished for bad luck
Idris Bakayoko is the clearest case in Europe this season. Four goals from 10.7 expected is the largest underperformance in our database outside the top five leagues. His chance creation, carrying volume (188 progressive carries) and shot quality are all comfortably above what his market valuation of €11.5m implies. He is not a bad finisher having a bad season — the sample of finishing is small and noisy, while the sample of *getting into positions* is large and stable. That second thing is the harder skill and he has it.

[Costeira Diário — 30 May 2026]
Estorilha coach defends Bakayoko: "He is doing the difficult part"
"Everyone wants to talk about the goals," said manager Rui Camposinho. "The goals will come. What he does before the goals — the running, the carrying, the getting there — that is the part you cannot teach. I would rather have a player who gets there and misses than one who never gets there."

[Transfer Signal — 21 June 2026]
No formal bids have been received for Bakayoko, whose contract runs to 2028. Estorilha are not thought to be actively seeking a sale.
