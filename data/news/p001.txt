[Bundeskreis Wochenblatt — 14 March 2026]
Osterwald confirm Petrovic surgery, out 4-5 months
FC Osterwald have confirmed that centre-back Milos Petrovic underwent surgery on his right knee this week, the second such procedure in eighteen months. Club doctor Wilhelm Sauer described the operation as "successful" but declined to give a firm return date, saying only that the 24-year-old would be reassessed at the end of the season. Petrovic missed 21 matches last campaign with a related issue.

[Transfer Signal — 2 May 2026]
Petrovic to Meridian? Three clubs monitoring, but medicals loom
At least three Meridian Premier clubs have registered interest in Milos Petrovic, whose performances when fit have marked him out as one of the better ball-playing defenders in Bundeskreis A. One recruitment source, speaking anonymously, was blunt: "Nobody doubts the player. Everybody doubts the knee. Any deal is going to be structured around appearance clauses, and he'll need to pass a very thorough medical."

[Osterwald club statement — 20 June 2026]
Petrovic resumes light training
Milos Petrovic has returned to light individual training at the club's Waldheim facility. The club asks supporters and media to be patient regarding a return-to-play timeline.

[Bundeskreis Wochenblatt — 30 June 2026]
Column: The Petrovic problem
There is a version of Milos Petrovic — the one who played 890 minutes this season at a level nobody else at Osterwald approached — who is worth every euro of his valuation. There is another version who has now spent more of his early twenties rehabilitating than playing. Buying him is not a scouting decision. It is a medical one, and it belongs to the doctors more than the analysts.
