Carvalho, 33, is still the best midfielder in this league
There is a temptation to write about Bruno Carvalho as a story about age. Resist it. Sancroix's captain has produced eight goals and twelve assists this season with a pass completion of 90.8% across nearly 2,800 minutes — numbers that would flatter a player ten years younger.

[Corvana Deportiva — 2 April 2026]
Carvalho contract talks break down
Bruno Carvalho will leave Real Sancroix at the end of June when his contract expires, the club have confirmed, ending an eight-year association. Sporting director Ana Belasco said the club had offered "terms appropriate to the player's age" and that Carvalho's camp had sought a longer commitment than Sancroix were prepared to give.

[Transfer Signal — 15 May 2026]
Free agent Carvalho: two good years or one?
The obvious appeal is the price. A player of this quality on a free transfer is a rare thing, and Carvalho's agent has indicated his client will prioritise a competitive squad over wages. The obvious risk is equally clear: he turns 34 in the autumn, and midfielders of that age tend to decline sharply rather than gently. Recruitment departments will differ on whether the answer is a two-year deal with an option, or no deal at all.
