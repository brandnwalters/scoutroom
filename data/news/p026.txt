[Noord Voetbal — 15 February 2026]
Osei's breakthrough interrupted
Nils Osei was making a strong case for a regular starting role at SC Heemskerk before a hamstring strain in December cost him six weeks. The 20-year-old has managed 1,150 minutes across the season and the club are keen to build on that next year.

[Transfer Signal — 11 June 2026]
Osei attracting interest but Heemskerk resistant
At €25.6m, SC Heemskerk's valuation of Nils Osei reflects potential rather than production. The club have indicated they would prefer another season of development before considering a sale, and the player's representatives have not pushed for a move. The hamstring issue is not considered recurrent.
