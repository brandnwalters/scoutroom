[Bundeskreis Wochenblatt — 2 February 2026]
Mitrovic dropped as Königsau change shape
Zoran Mitrovic was left out of the starting eleven for the third time in five matches as TSV Königsau shifted to a system requiring more mobility from the deeper midfield positions. The 31-year-old has started fewer than half of the club's league fixtures this season.

[Analytics Corner — 19 April 2026]
Quiet declines: players whose valuations haven't caught up
Zoran Mitrovic's minutes have fallen from 2,600 two seasons ago to 1,640 this year, and his output has fallen with them. His market valuation of €19m reflects the player he was rather than the player he is. Ageing defensive midfielders tend to lose ground physically before the market notices, and the market has not yet noticed.

[Transfer Signal — 18 June 2026]
Königsau open to offers for Mitrovic
TSV Königsau are understood to be willing to listen to offers for Zoran Mitrovic, whose contract expires in 2027 and whose wages are among the highest at the club. No serious interest has been reported.
