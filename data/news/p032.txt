[Vantina Calcio Settimanale — 28 January 2026]
Marchetti hits double figures for assists
Luca Marchetti's cut-back was converted at the near post to give AC Vitrano a 2-1 win, taking the playmaker to ten assists for the season. Vitrano value the 26-year-old at €44m and have described him as central to the project.

[Analytics Corner — 19 April 2026]
Assists are a team statistic: the Marchetti case
Thirteen assists is a lovely number. Six-point-one expected assists is a rather less lovely one. The gap is not a knock on Marchetti — it means his teammates finished the chances he created at a rate well above average, which is a thing that happens and then stops happening. Buyers paying €44m for the assist column should understand they are partly paying for the finishing of players who will not be joining him.

[Transfer Signal — 8 June 2026]
Vitrano set Marchetti price, no discount expected
AC Vitrano have privately indicated they will not negotiate below their €44m valuation, with the player under contract until 2030 and no pressure to sell. Interested clubs describe the asking price as "ambitious" but Vitrano's position is understood to be firm.
