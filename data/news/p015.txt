[Northgate FC academy bulletin — 22 March 2026]
Lindqvist scores twice off the bench
Nineteen-year-old Kai Lindqvist scored twice in eleven minutes after replacing the injured Andrej Radic, taking his tally to four in a season of largely substitute appearances. Manager Piet Osterhuis was measured afterwards: "He has scored four goals in a very small number of minutes. I would ask everyone, including Kai, to remember the second half of that sentence."

[Transfer Signal — 10 May 2026]
The €12m teenager: are clubs buying a highlight reel?
Kai Lindqvist has 340 league minutes to his name. Four of them produced goals. Extrapolated across a season that is a rate no striker in Europe sustains, and several clubs appear to be doing exactly that extrapolation. One analyst we spoke to was withering: "Three hundred and forty minutes tells you nothing. It's four coin flips. Ask again in two years."

[Northgate FC — 18 June 2026]
Lindqvist signs contract extension to 2030
Northgate have moved to secure their teenage forward on improved terms. The club say they intend to increase his minutes next season and have no plans to sanction a loan.
