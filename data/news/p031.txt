[Noord Voetbal — 9 February 2026]
Verhoeven tops passing charts for third straight season
Joris Verhoeven has again finished the campaign with the highest pass completion rate in the Eredivisie Noord, at 94.1%. The FC Vlaardam midfielder has now led the division in that metric three seasons running.

[Analytics Corner — 26 April 2026]
Pass completion is the most misleading statistic in football
Our worked example this month is Joris Verhoeven, who completes 94.1% of his passes and registered 21 progressive carries across 2,900 minutes — one of the lowest figures for any midfielder in our sample. A high completion rate achieved by passing sideways and backwards to unmarked teammates is not evidence of control; it is evidence of risk aversion. Verhoeven is a fine recycler of possession. He is not a progressor of it, and clubs shopping on the completion column should know which they are buying.

[Noord Voetbal — 14 June 2026]
Vlaardam coach on Verhoeven: "He does exactly what I ask"
"People say he passes backwards. Yes — because I tell him to. He is the safest player I have ever coached." Verhoeven, 29, has no injury history of note and is contracted until 2028.
