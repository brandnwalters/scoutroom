[Meridian Premier notebook — 5 January 2026]
Okafor reaches twenty
Emeka Okafor became the youngest player in six seasons to reach twenty league goals before the turn of the year, a milestone the Halewood City forward marked by immediately talking about the team. "Twenty is a number. Winning something is the thing," he told the broadcaster.

[Analytics Corner — 19 April 2026]
Okafor: the rare case where the price is the price
It is fashionable in our line of work to find the expensive player who is secretly a mirage. Emeka Okafor is not that. Twenty-one goals from 19.8 expected is essentially par — he is scoring what his chances merit, and he is generating those chances at elite volume across 2,830 minutes. There is no correction coming, because there is nothing to correct.

[Halewood City club statement — 1 June 2026]
Halewood have rejected an approach for Emeka Okafor and reiterate that the player is not for sale at any price this window. Okafor has three seasons remaining on his current deal.

[The Terrace Weekly — 26 June 2026]
Okafor's teammates on the striker: "Boring, in the best way"
Captain Amadou Ravelli, asked what makes Okafor difficult to play against, laughed. "He does the same thing every week. Every single week. It's boring to watch in training and it's a nightmare on Saturday." No injury record of note; Okafor has missed two league matches in three seasons.
