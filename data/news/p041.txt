[Bundeskreis Wochenblatt — 12 February 2026]
Vukovic's price tag draws scrutiny
Noah Vukovic's eleven goals from 12.4 expected represent a perfectly respectable season. His €57.4m valuation represents something else. VfL Brandthal's asking price is understood to be driven by a release clause negotiated when the striker signed in 2024, rather than by any assessment of current market value.

[Transfer Signal — 4 June 2026]
Brandthal will not negotiate below clause for Vukovic
There is no flexibility here: the clause is the price, and VfL Brandthal have no need to sell a 24-year-old contracted to 2030. Clubs that have enquired describe the figure as prohibitive relative to comparable strikers.
