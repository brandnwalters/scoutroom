Bocanegra: talent not in question, temperament is
Every scouting report on Nuno Bocanegra says the same two things. The first is that he is one of the most naturally gifted wingers in Serie Vantina. The second, usually further down, is that he has been disciplined internally at least twice, once following a training-ground altercation reported by local media in October. US Montebelli have not commented on either incident.
