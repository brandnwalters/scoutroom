[Meridian Youth Report — 25 January 2026]
Rasmussen, 18, is the most hyped teenager in the country
Kofi Rasmussen has five goals in 780 minutes for Ravensmoor FC and a valuation of €33.9m, a figure that says a great deal about what clubs believe he will become and rather less about what he has done. Ravensmoor have handled him carefully, limiting his starts and shielding him from press duties.

[Transfer Signal — 14 May 2026]
The teenage premium: what are you actually buying?
Kofi Rasmussen's price is not built on 780 minutes of football. It is built on projection — a bet that an 18-year-old with this physical profile becomes a €90m player at 23. Sometimes that bet pays extravagantly. Often it does not, and the club that made it is left holding a €34m squad player. Buyers should be clear with themselves about which product they are purchasing.

[Ravensmoor FC — 21 June 2026]
Ravensmoor state that Rasmussen is not for sale and will be integrated gradually into the first-team squad next season. No injury concerns are recorded.
