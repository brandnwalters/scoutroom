[Corvana Deportiva — 21 February 2026]
Serrano's quiet excellence
Diego Serrano does not do anything spectacular. He does everything well. Nine goals, ten assists, 164 progressive carries, 85.9% passing — there is no column in which Atlético Ondara's number ten is deficient, and no column in which he is the best in the league. Coaches love him. Highlight compilers do not.

[Transfer Signal — 12 May 2026]
Serrano attracting interest, Ondara open to €26m
Atlético Ondara are understood to be willing to sell Diego Serrano at around their €26m valuation, with the 25-year-old entering the peak years of his career and the club needing to fund a rebuild elsewhere in the squad. There are no complicating factors: the contract runs to 2029, the medical record is clean, and the player is said to be receptive to a move.
