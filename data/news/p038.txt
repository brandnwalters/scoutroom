Grimsson, 22, has played every minute
Anton Grimsson has completed 2,880 league minutes for Aalstrup BK this season — every available minute — while posting the highest tackle count of any defender in the Superliga Danica and a pass completion rate of 91.2%. He turned 22 in February.

[Transfer Signal — 3 June 2026]
Aalstrup brace for Grimsson bids as contract enters final year
Anton Grimsson's contract expires in the summer of 2027, meaning this is the last window in which Aalstrup BK can command a fee. The Danish club value him at around €6m, a figure several scouts have described as well below what a defender of his age and profile would cost in a wealthier league. Aalstrup have made no secret of their willingness to sell at the right price.

Grimsson: "I'm not in a hurry"
Asked about interest from abroad, Grimsson gave the standard answer and then, unusually, an honest one. "I would like to test myself higher. But I have played one full season. One. I don't think I've earned anything yet."
