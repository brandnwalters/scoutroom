[Kalorska Sport — 6 March 2026]
Diakite reaches double figures at 21
Fode Diakite scored his eleventh and twelfth of the season in NK Vratnica's win, and now has fourteen goals and eleven assists in a campaign that has made him comfortably the most-watched player in the division. He is 21 years old.

[Transfer Signal — 30 April 2026]
Why has nobody signed Fode Diakite?
The numbers are absurd for the price. Fourteen goals, eleven assists, 201 progressive carries, expected figures that back all of it up — and a valuation of €8.5m. Scouts we spoke to gave the same two reasons for the market's hesitancy, and neither concerned the player. The first is league quality: Liga Kalorska is not a division that regularly exports to the top five, and buyers apply a heavy discount to the numbers. The second is simpler and less defensible — very few clubs have anyone permanently watching that division at all.

[Kalorska Sport — 2 June 2026]
Vratnica reject approach, hold out for more
NK Vratnica have turned down an initial offer believed to be around €7m, with the club's president stating publicly that Diakite "will not leave for a price that embarrasses this club." The player is contracted until 2028, giving Vratnica no urgency.

[Kalorska Sport — 29 June 2026]
Diakite has not missed a match through injury in two seasons. Teammates describe him as unusually diligent for his age; his former youth coach at Vratnica's academy called him "the first one there, every day, no exceptions."
