[Bundeskreis Wochenblatt — 8 March 2026]
Ricci, 18, handed regular minutes
Finn Ricci has played 1,980 league minutes at the age of 18, an unusually heavy workload for a player of his age, and returned four goals and eight assists. VfL Brandthal have been praised for their patience and criticised, by some, for the physical demands placed on him.

[Transfer Signal — 26 May 2026]
€45.7m for a teenager: the projection premium in its purest form
Finn Ricci's output this season is decent, not remarkable. His valuation is remarkable. What buyers are paying for is the combination of age, minutes at a competitive level, and a technical profile that scouts uniformly rate. What they are not paying for is production, because there isn't much of it yet.

[VfL Brandthal — 19 June 2026]
Brandthal have publicly stated Ricci will not be sold this summer. No injury record of note; the player has been managed carefully and has missed no matches through fitness.
